Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Summary

clippers-ad runs only on its owner's computer. It has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

2. Data from TikTok

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, stored only in a local file on the owner's computer and used only to upload videos to that account's drafts (video.upload).

The app does not read the user's videos, followers, messages, or any other TikTok data.

3. Video content

Clips are created and stored locally on the owner's computer and are sent only to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), which makes the stored tokens stop working.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not offered to the public.
